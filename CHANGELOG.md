# Changelog

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

## 1.2.0 - 2026-11-18

### Changed

- Requires the 2.0 of its Laravel package, which runs on [laravel-privacy](https://github.com/jeffersongoncalves/laravel-privacy), the engine shared by the privacy suites. Same pages, settings and tables: nothing to change in your panel.

## 1.1.0 - 2026-11-16

- Translations for 17 more languages: ar, az, de, es, fa, fr, hi, it, ja, nl, pl, pt, ru, tr, uk, uz, zh_CN (19 in total with en and pt_BR)

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-16

Filament 3: consent ledger for [laravel-lgpd-consent](https://github.com/jeffersongoncalves/laravel-lgpd-consent) (LGPD art. 8).

- Consents of users and of visitors without an account
- Filters by purpose, terms version, decision and visitors
- Proof of each consent: IP address, user agent, source, metadata
- Grants for an older terms version flagged as no longer valid
- Read-only, like the append-only ledger
- English and Brazilian Portuguese
